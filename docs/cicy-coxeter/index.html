CICY Coxeter Database

Companion database to the paper Kaleidoscopes, Waves and the Prepotential by Rafael Álvarez-García and Fabian Ruehle. It extends the favorable-CICY dataset of Anderson–Gray–Lukas–Palti with the Coxeter-group data arising from isomorphic flops, surfacing the 4874 Kähler-favorable entries as a browseable explorer.

  • 7890CICYs total
  • 4874Kähler-favorable
  • 2182non-trivial Coxeter action
  • 251infinite-order

From the paper. Jump to CICY 6771 (hyperbolic \(I_2(\infty)\) — §3 running example), CICY 6971 (parabolic \(I_2(\infty)\)), or CICY 5528 (elliptic \(I_2(4)\)).

The full database is available in two formats:

  • Wolfram Mathematica (3.9 MB) — association list; the canonical form used in the companion paper.
  • Plain text (4.0 MB) — one record per block, one Key : Value per line.

Indices run 1,…,78901,\dots,78901,…,7890, matching the original CICY list of Candelas–Dale–Lütken–Schimmrigk; the 487448744874 Kähler-favorable entries carry a full Coxeter-data record, and the rest hold NonKahlerPos sentinels.

What do these columns mean?
ColumnTypeMeaning
NumintegerCICY index, 1–7890.
\(h^{1,1}\)integerHodge number.
\(h^{2,1}\)integerHodge number.
Fav.boolFavorable presentation (projective product) exists.
K-fav.boolKähler-favorable: Kähler and Mori cones descend from the ambient. Strictly stronger than Fav.; only K-favorable rows carry computed Coxeter data.
ProductboolDirect product of lower-dimensional Calabi–Yaus.
Rank(\(W\))integerRank of the iso-flop Coxeter group; — when the model is not Kähler-favorable.
Kindstringfinite | affine | indefinite | trivial | sentinel. The first three are paper-conventional; trivial = K-favorable with no iso-flop walls; sentinel = not K-favorable.
CoxeterstringConcise Coxeter-group label: Z2, I2(m) with m ∈ {2, 3, 4, P, H}, or — for higher rank — the canonical diagram-shape name (e.g. A1+B2).
ShapeKey*stringCanonical diagram-shape id (used by the Diagram-shape facet above and in the Parquet artefact).

Sentinel conventions. Non-Kähler-favorable rows hold NULL in Rank(W), Kind = sentinel, Coxeter = NonKahlerPos. Empty CoxeterMat = {} denotes the trivial case (no iso-flop walls); a single reflection \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) is encoded as {{1}}. In I2(m) labels, P and H both denote \(m=\infty\) but distinguish parabolic vs hyperbolic representations of the same abstract group.

* Not shown in the table; available via the Diagram-shape facet and the Parquet artefact at /cicy-coxeter/cicy-coxeter.parquet.
