CICY Coxeter Database

Companion database to Kaleidoscopes, Waves and the Prepotential (Álvarez-García & Ruehle). It extends the favorable CICY list of Anderson–Gao–Gray–Lee (2017), built on the original CICYs of Candelas–Dale–Lütken–Schimmrigk (1988), with data pertaining to the Coxeter symmetries that isomorphic flops induce on the Kähler moduli space.

Examples: CICY 6771 \([\mathrm{I}_2(\infty)\,(H)]\), CICY 6971 \([\mathrm{I}_2(\infty)\,(P)]\), CICY 5528 \([\mathrm{I}_2(4)]\).
